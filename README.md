# Sales-Performance-Dashboard
Power BI Sales Performance Dashboard for analyzing and visualizing sales data. Includes dataset cleaning (Python/Google Colab), data modeling, and interactive dashboards with KPIs, charts, and maps to derive strategic business insights.

📌 Overview
Interactive dashboard built in Power BI to analyze sales data and derive business insights.

⚙️ Features
Data cleaning in Google Colab (Python, Pandas, NumPy)

KPI Cards: Sales, Profit, Orders, Quantity

Charts: Sales by Category, Segment, Profit by Region, Monthly Trend, Top 10 Products

Map: Sales Distribution by State

📊 Insights
Technology leads revenue

Consumer segment dominates sales

West & East regions most profitable

November & December peak months

🛠️ Tech Stack
Python (Colab), Pandas, NumPy, Power BI
